# AXIS demo video runbook

## Before recording

- Use a dedicated demo account and fictional student details; do not show real profiles, scores, CVs, or private documents.
- Complete the profile and RIASEC assessment before recording so the video focuses on results instead of data entry.
- Check that the account has a saved assessment, career matches, and a roadmap. Prepare one short AI request only if the provider is responding normally.
- Close unrelated tabs and notifications. Use a clean browser profile at 1440×900, then capture one mobile view at 390px wide.
- Refresh the key pages once and confirm the demo account still shows its own profile and assessment history.

## Suggested 3–4 minute sequence

1. **Home (15–20s):** introduce AXIS as a tool for exploring strengths and career directions.
2. **Profile (30–40s):** show the fictional student's academic, interest, and activity information; explain that profile quality affects recommendations.
3. **RIASEC (25–35s):** show the completed Holland profile and explain how interests complement academic inputs.
4. **Matching (45–60s):** show the ranked career groups, compatibility percentages, reasons behind a score, and the largest gaps to work on.
5. **Roadmap (35–45s):** show a goal, weekly actions, and progress tracking connected to the selected career direction.
6. **AI/CV (optional, 20–30s):** show one AI result and the editable CV preview; describe AI as an optional assistant, not the source of the matching score.

## Recording checks

- Keep account email, browser autofill, API details, and private-document links out of frame.
- Avoid claims that a match score guarantees admission or employment; present it as guidance based on the profile and current model.
- If a page is slow or AI is unavailable, skip that segment and continue with the saved matching/roadmap results.
- After recording, review the full video for personal data, loading errors, and accidental exposure of browser or developer tools.
