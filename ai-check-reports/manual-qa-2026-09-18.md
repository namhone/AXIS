## Edge cases

The missing refresh-token, missing bearer-token, and malformed bearer-token
checks returned the expected responses:

- Missing refresh cookie: `401 REFRESH_TOKEN_REQUIRED`
- Missing Authorization header: `401 ACCESS_TOKEN_REQUIRED`
- Invalid bearer token: `401 INVALID_TOKEN`

Invalid registration payload checks were executed after the rate-limit flow and
therefore returned `429 RATE_LIMITED`. They must be repeated after the
15-minute limiter window or from a separate test IP to observe their validation
codes.
